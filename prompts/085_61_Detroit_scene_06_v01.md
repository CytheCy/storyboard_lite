# Scene 6: Conversation Between Neighbors

**Location:** Outside Pavel’s entry garden on the terrace ring  
**Time:** Day  
**Characters:** Lou, Pavel

---

```text
SHOT TYPE: Medium two-shot from waist up, slight low angle, conversational framing on a circular terrace ring in an underground atrium, depth showing multilevel walkways and hanging gardens behind

SUBJECT: Lou and Pavel, neighbors on Detroit’s underground terrace ring, paused outside Pavel’s small entry garden; Lou wears a patched cooling enviro-suit with a compact battery pack on the hip, light utility harness, and slim AR glasses; Pavel wears a clean but work-worn maintenance jumpsuit, fingerless haptic gloves, and heavier industrial goggles; both hold pocket-sized battery cases; Pavel’s door alcove is edged by a tidy micro-garden of mushrooms and herbs grown in stacked trays fed by reclaimed drip lines

FOREGROUND: The entry garden railing with salvaged metal posts and braided cable, small bio-lamps tucked among mossy planters, condensation beading on transparent irrigation tubes, a knee-high service bot idling with a scuffed shell and magnetic tool tray, a folded lightweight umbrella-shield for surface storms repurposed as a shade panel; a coiled extension cable plugged into a wall battery hub with exchange tags; fine traces of glittering particulate Cam Mist drifting in the air near Lou’s sleeve, subtly catching light

BACKGROUND: Prosperous Thrive atrium spanning multiple levels under a vast circular ceiling diffuser that simulates daylight; broad terrace roads curving around the void, lined with private residence entries, hanging gardens, and verdant planters; autonomous work bots tending vines on upper rings; tiny electric vehicles whisper along narrow transport corridors; glass-sided elevators glide between levels; below, an older, rougher district peeks through an open ramp—cracked concrete, exposed pipes, dripping utility lines, and a bustling market with improvised stalls; wall signage for “Battery Exchange” and “Fabrication Kiosk” in clean typography contrasts with hand-painted scavenger arrows

LIGHTING: Soft, even artificial daylight from the ceiling diffuser with subtle cool highlights; warm accent glow from bio-lamps and terrace sconces; specular flickers on condensation and Cam Mist motes; gentle rim light outlining Lou and Pavel against the atrium depth

ATMOSPHERE: Lived-in near-future resilience—quiet conversation amid distant market chatter and hum of ventilation; faint misty humidity in the air, drifting dust-cameras (Cam Mist) sparkling; the calm, secure feel of the Thrive ring juxtaposed with the grittier view below; sense of uneasy news traveling through a comfortable space

TEXTURE: Practical industrial construction—powder-coated rails, brushed aluminum panels, resin planters with scuffs, rubberized grips, braided cables, patched fabric on cooling suits, slightly corroded brackets, damp concrete with mineral streaks; lush leaf textures, mushroom caps with velvety surfaces; AR glasses and goggles with pristine lenses; bot shells with paint wear

NEGATIVE PROMPT: surface skyscraper skylines, open blue sky, flying cars, neon cyberpunk clichés, uniform ruin, uniform ultraclean futurism, medieval or fantasy elements, weapons, military armor, deserts, snowfields, gasoline vehicles, retro CRT clutter, excessive chrome, hologram billboards, anime proportions, fisheye distortion, overexposed highlights, muddy low-contrast, text overlays, logos, watermark

STYLE:
**Color palette**
- A tightly controlled teal-and-deep-green monochrome scheme dominates every frame, with near-black shadows and muted blue-gray midtones.
- Warm accents (burnt orange, mustard, cream, and the occasional red or coral) are used sparingly, so they pop: the orange protest signs, the red "BANNED" glow, the orange anatomy panel in the news graphic.
- The limited palette is what makes the set feel like one world, even across very different scenes.

**Linework and rendering**
- Confident, clean ink outlines with flat or lightly cel-shaded color fills, closer to comics than to painting.
- Subtle grain and texture overlays that give a printed, slightly aged feel, like risograph or screen-print work.
- Soft atmospheric gradients and glow effects (the hologram figure, the speed-swirl lines around the parkour scenes) layered over the flat shapes.

**Character design**
- Stylized but readable faces with simple features and expressive brows, in the vein of European *bande dessinée* or mid-century editorial illustration, rather than anime or hyper-realism.
- Recurring props like wraparound visor goggles act as a visual shorthand for the world's tech.
- Robots are sleek, matte, minimal.

**Composition**
- Dynamic, cinematic framing: dramatic leaps, crowd scenes with depth, and figures staged against detailed industrial or arena backgrounds.
- The news-broadcast scenes use a flatter, poster-like layout with panel grids, bold condensed sans-serif headlines, and cream borders, evoking WPA posters or vintage infographics.
```
