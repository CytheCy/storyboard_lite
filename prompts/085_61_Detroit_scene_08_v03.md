# Scene 8: Ad: Tray RB Launch

**Location:** Promotional announcement  
**Time:** N/A  
**Characters:** Narrator (Tray RB promo)

---

```text
SHOT TYPE: Wide promotional hero shot, three-quarter view at human height, slightly low angle to emphasize presence; balanced composition with product centered, lifestyle context around it.

SUBJECT: The Tray RB robot presented as a premium Thrive product in underground Detroit. The bot is human-proportioned with robust, practical industrial build; smooth composite chassis with subtle panel seams, joint geometry mirroring human biomechanics; adaptive multi-axis shoulders, articulated spine, and compliant ankles. Faceplate with soft-actuated brows, nostril-like intake vents, and moving cheek pads for expressivity; high-fidelity sensor array across brow and temples. Integrated rapid-mapping hardware visible as a compact LIDAR/radar/ultrasonic ring around the neck collar and hip belt; spectral scanners and material-ident sensors embedded in forearms and chest. Swappable battery pack docked at the lumbar with illuminated charge indicators. Finish is clean matte ceramic-gray with Thrive insignia and minimal orange service markers; no obvious salvage parts. The bot performs a home-care and logistics vignette: one arm lifts a compact battery crate; the other hand steadies a lightweight mobility rail, demonstrating supportive gait-assist stance. Its micro-movements read as decisively human, confident, and precise.

FOREGROUND: A tidy Thrive residential terrace threshold within Detroit’s underground atrium district. Circular terrace roadway passes close to the entry; a tiny electric vehicle idles curbside with side hatch open, its cargo bay lit and ready. The Tray RB stands on textured composite floor panels with embedded guidance LEDs. A low charging dock and a neat rack of high-density battery bricks sit to the left, tagged for exchange. On a small console table, AR glasses in two styles—sleek ultralight frames and sealed goggle variants—rest beside a palm-sized fab-printed tool. A narrow bundle of insulated cabling runs along the baseboard into a wall conduit marked “Thrive Grid Private.” Subtle floor drains hint at stormwater precautions.

BACKGROUND: Prosperous Thrive neighborhood carved into a vast multilevel underground atrium. Broad circular terrace roads stack across several levels, edged by railings draped with hanging gardens, ferns, and hydroponic vines irrigated via clear tubing. Artificial daylight projects from a luminous canopy lattice, producing soft skylike illumination over clean modern interiors. Opposite residences show curved doorways, sealed glass, and airlocks with pressure bars; a neighbor’s entry displays a discrete Thrive emblem. Farther back, a maintenance corridor reveals the city’s patchwork: older concrete ribs, retrofitted composite braces, and conduit bundles feeding smart panels. Tiny electric vehicles glide quietly along, and a couple of autonomous work bots trundle near a lift shaft. A distant market ring glows warmer, with stalls selling mushrooms and fresh greens. No surface skyline, only the layered, believable subterranean architecture of Detroit.

LIGHTING: Crisp artificial daylight from the atrium canopy, balanced with warm interior spill from the residence. Accent rim light traces the Tray RB’s silhouette, highlighting joint geometry and sensor arrays. Soft underglow from floor LEDs and vehicle cargo lighting defines depth and edges without harsh glare. Subtle reflections on the robot’s ceramic-matte finish; no specular hotspots.

ATMOSPHERE: Clean, well-filtered air on the terrace; a faint mist from garden irrigation catches light in the distance. Sense of calm order and quiet prosperity contrasted by the visible industrial bones of the city. Sound implied: gentle hum of fans, distant cart motors, water trickle from vertical gardens. Marketing tone: reassuring capability, independence, and dignity in later life.

TEXTURE: Contrasting materials—matte ceramic composites on the robot, soft elastomer at joints, brushed aluminum rail supports, smooth sealed concrete with anti-slip aggregate, living plant leaves with dew, clear irrigation tubing, and tidy cable looms. Subtle wear on terrace floor and railing caps to maintain realism; the Tray RB itself is pristine, launch-new, with zero smudges.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: surface skyscraper skylines, flying cars, neon cyberpunk clutter, uniformly ruined environments, uniform hyper-futurism, visible salvage parts on Tray RB, greasy grime on the bot, chaotic wiring, medieval or retro aesthetics, low-tech wooden interiors, desert landscapes, open sky, rainstorms indoors, excessive lens flares, over-saturated comic-book colors, fantasy creatures, weapons, police tape, branding other than Thrive, text overlays, human faces front and center, hospital death scene, gore, exaggerated bokeh, fish-eye distortion, off-theme signage.

STYLE:
Create a flat-colored, hand-drawn 2D comic book illustration with the visual simplicity of traditional animated television backgrounds and character designs.

Use bold, clean, uniform black outlines around characters, objects, and architectural features. Simplify all shapes into clear, readable graphic forms. Draw faces with stylized comic-book proportions, minimal facial detail, simple noses, expressive eyes, and flat skin colors.

Use strictly two-tone cel shading: one solid base color and one solid shadow color per surface. Shadows must have hard edges. No gradients, realistic reflections, soft shading, atmospheric perspective, or complex lighting effects.

Use a limited palette of muted teal, dark navy, dusty cyan, warm ochre, tan, and black. Colors should appear as solid, uniform fills, like traditional hand-painted animation cels.

Simplify environments aggressively. Represent complex machinery, tunnel infrastructure, pipes, and futuristic architecture with bold silhouettes and a few essential structural lines. Avoid dense mechanical detailing, crosshatching, tiny background objects, or intricate textures.

The entire image must resemble a frame from a traditionally drawn 2D animated science-fiction series, not a realistic graphic novel, detailed concept painting, video game screenshot, or 3D render.

**PRIORITY:** Readable shapes, bold outlines, flat colors, simplified anatomy, and unmistakably hand-drawn illustration. Stylization takes precedence over realism and environmental detail.
```
