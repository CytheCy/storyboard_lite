# Scene 3: Family Dinner and AR Two

**Location:** Family dining room in Rosewater  
**Time:** Evening  
**Characters:** Pavel, Mara, Gregor, Bettina, Zac, Lou (seen outside)

---

```text
SHOT TYPE:
- Interior wide shot, eye-level, slight three-quarter angle across an oval dining table, framing the whole family and capturing the view through large underground windows to the Rosewater terrace ring and distant atrium

SUBJECT:
- A family of five in Rosewater, a prosperous Thrive neighborhood deep beneath Detroit: Pavel at the head of the oval table in a loose blue shirt, mid-gesture with a fork; Mara with sharp jaw and large eyes, offering a sleepy grin; teens Gregor and Bettina trading glances; little Zac bumping the table with excitement
- Everyone wearing AR glasses (diverse styles: Mara’s are ultra-light and sleek; Pavel’s slightly thicker with subtle bone-conduction arms; Bettina’s translucent pastel rims; Gregor’s compact matte goggles; Zac’s rounded child-safe frames)
- Subtle AR Two visualizations bloom near Pavel’s head: faint, semi-transparent sound-geometry ribbons and cloudlike contours shimmering around corners and doorways, especially to a dark hallway beyond
- On the table: bowls and plates of stir-fry crem, glasses of water with condensation, a small shared platter, rechargeable cutlery warmer puck, a slim battery pack sitting in a ceramic cradle

FOREGROUND:
- The oval dining table surface: clean modern composite with soft grain, a fabric runner with animated leaf motifs echoing the wall; chopsticks and forks laid casually; a small portable lantern-style rod light centerpiece with warm LEDs; a hand-sized autonomous crumb bot parked idle near a napkin
- Bettina’s fingers touching her glasses as she says “I can see clouds,” with a faint AR cloudlet reflection in her lenses
- Pavel leaning back, fork paused midair, hands partially spread while describing “seeing sounds,” the movement captured as a momentary AR echo contour mapping the edge of his gesture
- Zac’s little hand thumping the table, causing an AR ripple that traces the rim of his glass

BACKGROUND:
- Rosewater interior architecture: a tall-ceilinged, multilevel subterranean residence with broad circular terrace roads visible through panoramic windows; beyond, the enormous underground atrium with hanging gardens, lush vegetation, and suspended walkways

- A shadowed hallway entrance off to one side inside the home, where AR Two’s faint outlines make doorframes and corners glow with gauzy, sound-reactive geometry

LIGHTING:
- Warm artificial daylight from rod lights above, balanced by soft, indirect wall lighting; gentle reflections on polished tableware
- Window light is cool and diffuse, filtered through the atrium’s artificial sky panels; hanging garden greenery casts soft dappled shadows

STYLE:
**Color palette**
- A tightly controlled teal-and-deep-green monochrome scheme dominates every frame, with near-black shadows and muted blue-gray midtones.
- Warm accents (burnt orange, mustard, cream, and the occasional red or coral) are used sparingly, so they pop: the orange protest signs, the red "BANNED" glow, the orange anatomy panel in the news graphic.
- The limited palette is what makes the set feel like one world, even across very different scenes.

**Linework and rendering**
- Confident, clean ink outlines with flat or lightly cel-shaded color fills, closer to comics than to painting.
- Subtle grain and texture overlays that give a printed, slightly aged feel, like risograph or screen-print work.
- Soft atmospheric gradients and glow effects (the hologram figure, the speed-swirl lines around the parkour scenes) layered over the flat shapes.

**Character design**
- Stylized but readable faces with simple features and expressive brows, in the vein of European *bande dessinée* or mid-century editorial illustration, rather than anime or hyper-realism.
- Recurring props like wraparound visor goggles act as a visual shorthand for the world's tech.
- Robots are sleek, matte, minimal.

**Composition**
- Dynamic, cinematic framing: dramatic leaps, crowd scenes with depth, and figures staged against detailed industrial or arena backgrounds.
- The news-broadcast scenes use a flatter, poster-like layout with panel grids, bold condensed sans-serif headlines, and cream borders, evoking WPA posters or vintage infographics.
```
