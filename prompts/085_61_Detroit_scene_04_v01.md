SHOT TYPE:
- Medium-wide interior family dining scene framed from inside a prosperous underground Thrive residence, angled toward a panoramic window that looks onto the multilevel terrace ring; depth shows both the dinner table action and Lou walking outside with three tiny electric cars of mushroom bins.

SUBJECT:
- A mixed-age family at a sleek dining table in Detroit’s underground Thrive neighborhood: Pavel (middle-aged) raising a glass; Mara leaning toward an A.R. display; Bettina suppressing a laugh; Zac beaming while gesturing mid-air A.R. panels; a small wire rat cage on a chair with a calm pet rat “Snow.” Everyone wears distinctive AR glasses or goggles. Zac’s lightweight goggles project semi-transparent charts and rat photos above the table. Clothing blends casual fabrics with cooling-thread accents typical of comfortable Thrive interiors.

FOREGROUND:
- Polished composite dining table with bioplastic plates, a sliced carrot fruit glistening; reusable metal cutlery; a compact battery-charging tray with power cells; low bonsai-like mushroom centerpiece nodding to local growers; slimline AR projectors recessed along the table edge.
- A wire cage on the chair next to Zac with soft bedding, water pipette, and a smart tag clipped to the door; the rat Snow visible, glossy coat.
- Subtle table-edge safety rails common to terrace homes. Small personal cooling packs clipped to chair backs.

BACKGROUND:
- Through a broad window wall, a clean, circular terrace road ringed around a vast multilevel atrium typical of Thrive areas: layered walkways, broad ramps, and glass elevators descending into deeper levels. Hanging gardens and lush vertical planters cascade from higher terraces; soft artificial daylight panels above emulate evening warmth.
- Lou in an older enviro-suit with scuffed knee pads and a slightly stooped posture, walking instead of riding, followed by three tiny electric cargo pods loaded with stacked mushroom bins. Their indicator LEDs blink low-power blue; side panels show co-op grower stencils. A maintenance bot trundles along the far railing. A battery exchange kiosk glows discreetly on the terrace.
- Beyond: clean modern interiors and residences with private entryway gardens—except Lou’s minimal, plantless doorway. Occasional distant figures in cooling suits and stylish AR eyewear; terraced vegetation contrasts with visible service conduits routed neatly along walls.

LIGHTING:
- Warm artificial “evening broadcast” key light from ceiling panels and under-shelf strips inside the dining room; soft bounce off the table.
- Cooler ambient from the atrium outside, diffused through the window, giving a gentle rim light to faces and AR graphics. Subtle reflections of the terrace lights on the glass.

ATMOSPHERE:
- Comfortable, prosperous calm indoors contrasted with diligent work-life realism outside. Air visibly clean inside; faint humidity and mist diffusers visible in the atrium around hanging gardens. A sense of civic functionality with gentle broadcast-time hush.

TEXTURE:
- Interior finishes: clean composite surfaces, matte ceramic tableware, breathable high-thread fabrics with micro-mesh cooling panels, transparent AR overlays crisp and legible.
- Exterior: smooth terrazzo terrace, hydroponic planters with dewy leaves, scuffed enviro-suit fabric, rugged plastic mushroom bins, brushed metal railings, tidy conduit runs.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
- No surface skyline, no skyscrapers or flying cars, no uniformly ruined wasteland, no hyper-futuristic neon cityscape, no medieval or rustic decor, no fantasy creatures, no weapons, no excessive grime inside Thrive home, no 20th-century CRTs, no holograms replacing AR glasses, no empty faceless mannequins, no generic high-tech labs, no cluttered cyberpunk signage, no rain indoors, no dystopian riot scenes, no anachronistic candles or chandeliers, no missing AR glasses on characters.

STYLE:
**Color palette**
- A tightly controlled teal-and-deep-green monochrome scheme dominates every frame, with near-black shadows and muted blue-gray midtones.
- Warm accents (burnt orange, mustard, cream, and the occasional red or coral) are used sparingly, so they pop: the orange protest signs, the red "BANNED" glow, the orange anatomy panel in the news graphic.
- The limited palette is what makes the set feel like one world, even across very different scenes.

**Linework and rendering**
- Confident, clean ink outlines with flat or lightly cel-shaded color fills, closer to comics than to painting.
- Subtle grain and texture overlays that give a printed, slightly aged feel, like risograph or screen-print work.
- Soft atmospheric gradients and glow effects (the hologram figure, the speed-swirl lines around the parkour scenes) layered over the flat shapes.

**Character design**
- Stylized but readable faces with simple features and expressive brows, in the vein of European *bande dessinée* or mid-century editorial illustration, rather than anime or hyper-realism.
- Recurring props like wraparound visor goggles act as a visual shorthand for the world's tech.
- Robots are sleek, matte, minimal.

**Composition**
- Dynamic, cinematic framing: dramatic leaps, crowd scenes with depth, and figures staged against detailed industrial or arena backgrounds.
- The news-broadcast scenes use a flatter, poster-like layout with panel grids, bold condensed sans-serif headlines, and cream borders, evoking WPA posters or vintage infographics.
