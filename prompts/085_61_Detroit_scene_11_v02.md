# Scene 11: Bettina Heads to the Canal Club

**Location:** Freight elevator to underground club; canal club  
**Time:** Night  
**Characters:** Bettina, Elise, Berto, Crowd

---

```text
SHOT TYPE: Wide three-quarter view from inside a rattling industrial freight elevator cage, slightly low angle, framing Bettina, Elise, and Berto with the elevator gate open to reveal the underground canal club entrance beyond.

SUBJECT: Bettina at front in a sleek cooling suit with integrated micro-fans and ultra-light AR glasses, hair pinned back with a battery clip; Elise beside her in a tailored Thrive-grade enviro-jacket with soft-armor panels and minimalist goggles; Berto behind them in a patched work jumper and bulky salvage-grade goggles, lugging a rechargeable battery crate. They’re descending to the Canal Club—an underground venue built into the buried Detroit canal works—anticipation and tension on their faces, AR UI ghosting on their lenses.

FOREGROUND: Scuffed metal elevator floor with stenciled load limits, a mesh safety gate half-slid open, grease-streaked hydraulic pistons, dangling call-panel with cracked plastic, a salvage bot the size of a carry-on humming at ankle height with taped-on Li-ion packs, orange safety webbing, and a humming rack of swapped community batteries with hand-etched IDs. Sparse puddles vibrate from the elevator’s brake chatter, reflecting hazard stripes and dim utility bulbs.

BACKGROUND: The Canal Club carved into old Detroit canal infrastructure: a broad concrete vault with curved, water-dark walls, original riveted steel ribs, and newly cut terrace walkways circling a narrow, slow, ink-black water channel. Improvised neon signage “CANAL CLUB” welded to an ancient beam, letters flickering from uneven power. Crowd queues along a mud-stained catwalk: scavengers in patched cooling suits, mushroom growers with spore-proof hoods, traders in grimy vests, a few Thrive members in crisp attire—all wearing diverse AR glasses or goggles. Overhead, a tangle of conduit and reclaimed fiber lines snakes along the ceiling, with condensation beading and dripping into catch-trays. Tiny electric taxis nose along a narrow ramp, their tires wet, quietly swapping passengers and battery packs. Autonomous work bots ferry kegs and crates via rail-guides, while a homemade DJ booth nests under a hanging garden pocket—tough, leafy vines irrigated by drip lines and grow LEDs—hinting at Thrive prosperity leaking into a rough district. Security stands on a grated balcony: two bots with adaptive grips and a human bouncer in a reinforced enviro-coat, verifying wrist tags and battery credits. Farther back, a spill of warm light from an arched entry reveals the club interior: multilevel terraces stacked around the canal, tables bolted to concrete, low mist over the water, and a repurposed pump-turbine stage where a performer tests a mic among coiled hoses.

LIGHTING: Mixed-source underground night. Harsh sodium-amber utility bulbs line the elevator and corridor, cool-white LEDs trace safety paths, and moody aquamarine uplights graze the canal water. Neon signage flickers magenta and cyan over damp concrete; occasional strobe glints from within the club. AR overlays softly glow on everyone’s glasses. Reflections shimmer in puddles and on wet rebar. Backlight silhouettes the crowd, rim-lighting Bettina’s profile and Elise’s jacket edges.

ATMOSPHERE: Humid, metallic air; faint mist hugging the canal; distant bass thump from inside; elevator brake-dust smell mixing with damp concrete, mushroom substrate, ozone from chargers, and fry oil from a food stall. Voices layer in overlapping echoes; the wind howls faintly through old ventilation shafts, a reminder of lethal surface storms above.

TEXTURE: Cracked concrete, limescale streaks, chipped paint, and rubberized anti-slip strips; pitted steel ribs slick with condensation; patched fabric of cooling suits and matte ceramic armor plates; plastic battery shells scuffed and stickered; vine leaves glossy under grow lights; water surface oily-smooth with ripples from passing bots; gritty mud edging the catwalk.
```
