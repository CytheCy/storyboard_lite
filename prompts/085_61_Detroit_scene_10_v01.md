# Scene 10: Gregor’s Music and P-Clone Shift

**Location:** Upcycle market; family home; virtual jam spaces  
**Time:** Various (months later; late night)  
**Characters:** Gregor, Mara, P-Gregor (P-clone)

---

```text
SHOT TYPE: Medium-wide three-panel composite: left panel shows a dim, crowded upcycle market aisle underground; center panel shows a modest family home alcove; right panel shows an overlapping virtual jam space bleeding into both, AR overlays visible through everyone’s diverse glasses.

SUBJECT: Gregor, Mara, and P-Gregor (P-clone). Gregor wears a scuffed cooling suit with cracked polymer plates, a soft-neck evaporative collar, and lightweight AR glasses with tethered battery pack; a salvaged micro-synth slung at his hip. Mara, in a faded house wrap with embedded cooling tubing and translucent, wide-lens AR goggles, stands shaking, eyes red. P-Gregor appears as a sharply lit volumetric avatar, slightly translucent at the edges, mimicking Gregor’s face but with smoother skin and perfectly steady posture; he wears ultra-lightweight frameless AR glasses. A muted doctor avatar hovers near Mara, hands to thighs, speech visually indicated but unheard.

FOREGROUND: Left: bins of salvaged PCB boards, tangled wiring looms, repurposed battery caddies with exchange stickers, dripping condensation tubed to buckets; a tiny electric cargo trike loaded with sealed battery bricks; a tray of hand-grown oyster mushrooms for trade. Center: a low table with a ceramic water recycler, a cracked family photo frame, two folded cooling blankets, and a compact med-bot dock with indicator LEDs at 12% battery. Right: a floating virtual mixer UI—glowing faders, looping icons, latency readout—intersecting reality; a translucent virtual drum pad hovering over the trike’s cargo bed; music track names tagged “Gregor’s Pulse,” “P-Gregor Latency Comp,” and a muted channel labeled “Zac.”

BACKGROUND: Detroit’s subterranean patchwork—left panel opens into an impoverished upcycle market corridor with exposed pipes, rusted beam supports wrapped in improvised struts, stained concrete walls with arrowed stencil signage to “Terrace Lifts” and “Battery Exchange,” utility lights flickering through humidity, traders in mixed enviro-suits bartering under tarps. Center panel reveals a modest family home carved from an old maintenance bay: mud-boot rack, wall-mounted coolant radiator, a fabric partition leading to a compact sleeping nook; ceiling condensation lines feeding a graywater barrel; a secondhand care-bot chassis covered by a towel; a simple shrine shelf with a child’s drawing and a tiny LED candle. Right panel hints at a distant Thrive atrium via AR window: enormous multilevel terraces, hanging gardens, smooth circular roads, soft artificial daylight—clean geometry intentionally contrasted with the gritty real space; on an upper terrace, a sleek Tray RB care robot demonstrates fluid joint articulation beside lush vegetation and glass balustrades, tagged with data overlays and Thrive’s emblem.

LIGHTING: Mixed-source lighting. Real space is dim, sodium-warm utility LEDs and scattered cool-white task lamps, broken by pockets of darkness and shiny puddle reflections; thin shafts of fan-stirred mist catch light. Virtual elements cast cool, crisp rim glows on nearby edges—P-Gregor lit in even, studio-like daylight; the doctor avatar pale and clinical; virtual jam UI radiating cyan and magenta highlights.

ATMOSPHERE: Humid, metallic air with audible drip patterns and the soft whir of fans; market murmur and clatter underlaid by a gentle, glitching metronome from the jam space. Emotional tension palpable—Mara’s tremor, Gregor’s tight jaw; the P-clone’s calm presence both comforting and uncanny. The storm world implied through warning tickers in AR: surface winds severe, temperature spikes, rain surge alerts.

TEXTURE: Wet concrete sheen, pitted and cracked; flaking paint; frayed insulation on pipes; muddy footprints; rubberized cooling fabrics beaded with condensation; smooth, polished composites on P-Gregor’s avatar edges; Tray RB in the AR atrium gleaming with premium ceramic-coated metal; mushrooms velvety; battery bricks scraped and stickered; UI elements razor-sharp and luminous against the grit.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: sunny surface cityscapes, conventional skyscraper skylines, flying cars, uniform neon cyberpunk streets, glossy utopia without grime, medieval/fantasy elements, steampunk gears, retro 1980s sci-fi chrome, deserts or dunes, open blue skies, drones swarming overhead, holograms as primary light source only, crowded uniform high-tech megacity, ruined wasteland with no tech, vintage CRT clutter, aliens or non-human creatures, weapons or military gear, medieval armor, rainbow lens flares, excessive bloom, plastic doll faces, overly saturated cartoon colors, low-detail backgrounds, missing AR glasses on characters

STYLE:
**Color palette**
- A tightly controlled teal-and-deep-green monochrome scheme dominates every frame, with near-black shadows and muted blue-gray midtones.
- Warm accents (burnt orange, mustard, cream, and the occasional red or coral) are used sparingly, so they pop: the orange protest signs, the red "BANNED" glow, the orange anatomy panel in the news graphic.
- The limited palette is what makes the set feel like one world, even across very different scenes.

**Linework and rendering**
- Confident, clean ink outlines with flat or lightly cel-shaded color fills, closer to comics than to painting.
- Subtle grain and texture overlays that give a printed, slightly aged feel, like risograph or screen-print work.
- Soft atmospheric gradients and glow effects (the hologram figure, the speed-swirl lines around the parkour scenes) layered over the flat shapes.

**Character design**
- Stylized but readable faces with simple features and expressive brows, in the vein of European *bande dessinée* or mid-century editorial illustration, rather than anime or hyper-realism.
- Recurring props like wraparound visor goggles act as a visual shorthand for the world's tech.
- Robots are sleek, matte, minimal.

**Composition**
- Dynamic, cinematic framing: dramatic leaps, crowd scenes with depth, and figures staged against detailed industrial or arena backgrounds.
- The news-broadcast scenes use a flatter, poster-like layout with panel grids, bold condensed sans-serif headlines, and cream borders, evoking WPA posters or vintage infographics.
```
