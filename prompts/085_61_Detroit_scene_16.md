# Scene 16: Bettina Exposed to Slip

**Location:** Canal club porta potties; Sixty-Five Tube; Bettina’s bedroom  
**Time:** Night  
**Characters:** Bettina, Elise, Thrive (AI)

---

```text
SHOT TYPE:
- Tight mid-shot, club dancefloor perspective with shallow depth of field, rapid strobe freeze-frames on faces and movement, slight Dutch angle to amplify unease.

SUBJECT:
- Bettina, mid-20s, sweating and breathless in a patched cooling suit repurposed as clubwear: matte-black lightweight fabric with visible seam tape, microfan vents at collarbones, translucent battery pouch clipped at her hip glowing faint cyan; augmented-reality glasses pushed up into her dark hair. Her hands are raised as she dances; expression wavering between exhilaration and alertness. A brief, vivid memory of the man with regrown cheek skin and horizontally narrowed eyes is implied as a strobe-ghost behind her shoulder. Elise presses in at Bettina’s side in an iridescent silver dress layered over a thin enviro baselayer with cooling veins, laughing, hair damp, AR contact lenses catching the strobe. A narrow white beam strikes Bettina’s shirt, scattering tiny multicolor point reflections across her chest for a single frame.

FOREGROUND:
- Sweaty shoulders and backs of other dancers in improvised underground Detroit clubwear: mesh-panel cooling tops, salvaged harnesses with clipped lithium packs, flexible respirator masks pulled down to throats, illuminated scarf whipping arcs of soft neon, ribbons wrapped around a fem’s arms trailing micro-LEDs. A tiny dockless electric scooter knocked sideways near a grated floor edge; a scavenged battery crate used as a drink shelf with condensation pooling; a homemade wrist console flashing low battery icons. Water drips from a pipe seam above, captured mid-fall as bright beads in the strobe. A hand with chipped reflective polish catches Bettina’s waist, tugging her deeper into the cluster. The fleeting negative space where the unsettling man stood is now filled by a dancer with the glowing scarf, hands high.

BACKGROUND:
- Canal club porta potties reimagined as a subterranean venue module along the Sixty-Five Tube: a converted maintenance corridor beside an old stormwater canal, with portable sanitation units lined in a row like cubicles, doors repainted with DIY murals and QR barter stickers. Cracked concrete walls, exposed corroded rebar, bundles of color-coded conduit and fiber lines snake along ceiling brackets; condensation streaks and mineral blooms. Industrial catwalks and grated platforms over dark, slow canal water, with sump pumps humming. Stenciled wayfinding: “65 TUBE — LOW DECK — DRAIN 14.” Improvised gantries anchor secondhand strobe rigs, foggers, and a rotating narrow-beam spotlight. A vendor corner: thermos coolers, electrolyte pouches, clip-on fan refills, battery exchange rack with numbered cells trading hands. A service robot with mismatched panels trundles past, carrying mop gear and a spill sensor; a pair of autonomous work bots idle at a charging plate with indicator LEDs. In glimpses through a security mesh, a narrow transit corridor pulses with the soft whirr of tiny electric vehicles. Far beyond, a polished elevator door bears a Thrive neighborhood logo, hinting at the wealthy levels above. No skylines; everything tunneled, layered, and lived-in.

Occasional green “Egress” strips and the faint biolum of DIY grow-moss patches add low-level glow.

ATMOSPHERE:

Sound implied: bass thumps causing ripples in puddles, the rattle of old vents, muffled pump throbs. Scent mix: ozone from lighting rigs, sweat, synthetic cologne, damp concrete. Tension threaded through celebration; Bettina’s hyper-awareness despite motion.

TEXTURE:

Everything tactile, practical, and near-future industrial.
