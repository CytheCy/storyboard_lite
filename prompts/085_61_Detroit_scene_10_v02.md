# Scene 10: Gregor’s P-Clone Evolves

**Location:** Upcycle market; home; virtual jam spaces  
**Time:** Months to years later (montage)  
**Characters:** Gregor, Mara, P-Gregor (P-clone)

---

```text
SHOT TYPE: Montage sequence board, alternating medium wides and close-ups, dynamic time-lapse beats across months to years, intercut between an underground upcycle market, a modest family home alcove, and virtual jam spaces, all within subterranean Detroit

SUBJECT: Gregor and his P-clone (P-Gregor) evolving from rough salvage assembly to confident co-creator; Mara present in quieter beats; background presence of Zac’s medical crisis conveyed through subtle AR notifications. Gregor wears a patched cooling suit with scuffed knee panels, compact battery bandolier, and lightweight AR glasses; P-Gregor begins as a bare composite hume-noid torso with printed lattice musculature and salvaged actuation pods, gradually gaining smoother panels and expressive facial servo meshes; both carry portable synth modules and cable rolls reclaimed from old factories

FOREGROUND: 
- Beat 1 (Upcycle market): Crates of scavenged servos, battery bricks marked with trade stencils, coiled coolant tubing dripping into catch trays, a table of 3D-printed joint sockets; Gregor adjusts a jaw actuator on P-Gregor while a tiny electric pallet rover hums past carrying labeled cells
- Beat 2 (Home): Low wooden bench beside a concrete support, steaming mugs atop a heat-reclaimer, Mara’s hands folded around a battery warmer; P-Gregor seated cross-legged, learning finger positions on a hacked string pad; AR overlays float like dim ghosts above their glasses, showing timing grids and biofeedback
- Beat 3 (Virtual jam space): Gregor and P-Gregor in head-tilted focus, ultra light weight AR frames projecting shared morphing instruments; fingertip haptics wired to printed gloves; a salvaged robot drummer on a stand clacks softly
- Beat 4 (Market again, later): P-Gregor trades a repaired micro-fabricator nozzle for rare cooling gel; stacks of mushroom substrate bags nearby; a fixer counts battery credits
- Beat 5 (Home, late): Mara watching from a doorway in a simple enviro-gown, eyes rimmed, a muted medical status ribbon scrolling on her AR; P-Gregor refines vibrato on a ribbon controller, Gregor nods, proud and worried

BACKGROUND:
- Upcycle market: Dim, humid thoroughfare carved from old transit tunnels; cracked concrete, exposed rebar, hanging conduit bundles, moisture beading on pipes; stalls built from pallet wood and sheet steel; improvised signage lit by mismatched utility lights; narrow transport corridor where tiny electric carts shuttle battery crates; scavengers and traders in mixed enviro-suits and cooling vests, all wearing diverse AR glasses or goggles; a salvaged loader-bot with patched plating negotiates puddles
- Home: Compact family alcove embedded in an older basement grid; patched wall insulation, thermal curtains over a narrow ramp; a ceiling fan on low, running off a wall battery stack with hand-scribed charge markers; shelf of seed-grade mushrooms, a pot of hardy greens under a grow-strip; child-height chalk marks on a pipe; sound-damp blankets hang to muffle tunnel noise
- Virtual jam spaces (projected, not literal rooms): Atrium-scale Thrive environment simulated in AR—broad circular terraces, skeletal bridges, lush hanging gardens, and artificial daylight shafts glinting; overlays of clean acoustic panels and floating notation while the real-world space remains modest; occasional pop-in of Thrive users as translucent silhouettes with pristine clothing and sleek AR eyewear
- City connective tissue: Elevators and ramps in the distance, narrow transport arteries, occasional view through a grille into a prosperous Thrive atrium with multilevel greenery and soft white surfaces, contrasting with the market’s grime

LIGHTING: Mixed-source; market lit by flickering sodium utility strips, battery lanterns, and stray welding arcs; home warmed by a soft LED strip and a cool-blue grow light; virtual jam overlays introduce bright, even “artificial daylight” glows that rim-light faces; reflective puddles add specular texture; intermittent blackout moments with only AR UI glows and headset reflections signal power scarcity

ATMOSPHERE: Humidity from constant seepage; faint vapor halos around coolant lines; drifting dust motes in the market; low fog from chilled air recyclers near the home floor; gentle leaf-scent from mushroom racks; occasional spray bursts from overhead leak manifolds; audio sense of distant pumps, fans, and tiny EVs; emotional current: worry for Zac woven through creative collaboration, resilience, and learning

TEXTURE: Gritty, damp concrete; flaking paint; condensation streaks; patched rubber matting; 3D-printed sanded plastics against raw lattice composites on P-Gregor; braided fiber cables fraying at ends; soft woven thermal blankets; smooth, high-finish Thrive surfaces in AR contrast with practical industrial materials in reality; skin sheen from heat and cooling suit contact

NEGATIVE PROMPT: surface skylines, flying cars, generic neon cyberpunk streets, uniform ultraclean sci-fi labs, medieval or steampunk motifs, glossy megacity towers, bright midday sun, deserts, forests, beaches, gasoline muscle cars, medieval armor, fantasy creatures, uniform ruin with no tech, all-chrome androids, vintage CRT clutter, excessive bloom, chaotic color grading, anachronistic fashion without AR glasses, unrealistic weaponry, overt warfare scenes, space backgrounds

STYLE:
Use a consistent stylized sci-fi graphic novel look: 2D editorial illustration, hand-inked outlines, simplified anatomy and faces, flat cel shading, posterized light and shadow, muted teal/cyan/navy palette with warm tan-orange accents, subtle printed-paper or screenprint texture, and strong clean silhouettes. Keep everything clearly drawn and illustrative, not photorealistic, not cinematic realism, and not a 3D render.
```
