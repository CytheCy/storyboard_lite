# Scene 4: Crime Catchers: Jack-Casting

**Location:** Crime news program  
**Time:** Night (broadcast)  
**Characters:** Host/Narrator

---

```text
SHOT TYPE: Medium-wide studio shot framed like a live night broadcast, anchored desk at center with over-shoulder screen insert, subtle parallax of AR overlays floating between camera and host

SUBJECT: Crime Catchers: Jack-Casting host/narrator seated at a recycled composite news desk fashioned from polished scrap aluminum and reclaimed subway glass, wearing a crisp, dark cooling suit with microtubing seams visible and a slim neck radiator, augmented-reality glasses with a sleek ultralight frame glowing faint blue at the temples; he gestures toward a floating AR package showing a candid still of “Lou” on a terrace ring overloading a tiny electric cargo car with towering mushroom harvest bins, plus a panel of biometric charts of “immortal” pet rats; a discreet desktop mic array, portable battery bricks with LED readouts, and a tangle of fiber and power couplers sit on the desk; the host’s expression mixes dry humor and concern as if narrating a cautionary segment

FOREGROUND: Transparent AR ticker tape scrolling crime tips and neighborhood alerts in blocky utilitarian fonts; a handheld gimbal camera drone the size of a fist hovers low-left with anti-dust grill; a cracked ceramic mug filled with electrolyte tea leaves condensation rings on the desk; a thin, weathered press badge with NFC tag hangs from the desk edge; spare battery cells labeled with barcodes and thumb-scorched tape; a rat-size enclosure briefly pictured on the AR insert, not physically present on the desk

BACKGROUND: Detroit’s underground Thrive broadcast bay carved into a former train concourse now enclosed as a multilevel atrium—broad circular terrace roads spiral behind glass partitions; hanging gardens trail moss and leafy vines from catwalk planters; clean, pale composite walls with embedded light panels simulate daylight; a panoramic window slot reveals, below, a poorer ring: cracked concrete, exposed pipes beaded with condensation, dripping ceilings, muddy passageways with pop-up market stalls and salvaged robots trundling; a narrow transport corridor carries two tiny electric vehicles, one lopsided under a precarious tower of rectangular mushroom bins, straps cinched, a wheel nearly lifting; autonomous work bots ferry battery crates; a vertical elevator shaft glows with safety LEDs; distant residents in varied enviro-suits and cooling vests all wearing different glasses or goggles

LIGHTING: Soft, artificial “daylight” panels wash the studio with a neutral key; rim lights pick out the host’s shoulders and the AR glasses edges; cooler, green-tinted spill from the poorer ring below adds contrast; small practicals—industrial utility lamps—blink amber status along pipe runs; screen light flickers subtly on the host’s hands

ATMOSPHERE: Dry, climate-controlled studio air with faint suspended dust motes caught in backlight; moisture haze and drifting mist visible in the lower, improvised district beyond the glass; a low hum of fans and battery inverters; on-screen lower-thirds animate with crisp transitions as if mid-broadcast; tension between polished news space and gritty city layers

TEXTURE: Practical industrial finishes—matte composites, brushed scrap metal seams, textured anti-slip decking, cable looms organized but visibly patched; AR graphics crisp and semi-transparent with light bloom; damp concrete and corroded pipe skin in the distance; lush, waxy plant leaves in hanging gardens; scuffed plastic on the tiny EVs; host’s cooling suit smooth with faint microchannel ribbing

NEGATIVE PROMPT: surface city skylines, flying cars, uniform megacity futurism, medieval or steampunk props, glossy utopian perfection, total ruin, neon cyberpunk rain-slick streets, bulky 20th-century CRTs, paper clutter, gasoline vehicles, weapon displays, superhero costumes, exaggerated anime proportions, fantasy creatures, deserts, snowy landscapes, sunlight through clouds above ground, gothic arches, holograms unattached to AR devices, missing glasses on people

STYLE:
Use a consistent stylized sci-fi graphic novel look: 2D editorial illustration, hand-inked outlines, simplified anatomy and faces, flat cel shading, posterized light and shadow, muted teal/cyan/navy palette with warm tan-orange accents, subtle printed-paper or screenprint texture, and strong clean silhouettes. Keep everything clearly drawn and illustrative, not photorealistic, not cinematic realism, and not a 3D render.
```
