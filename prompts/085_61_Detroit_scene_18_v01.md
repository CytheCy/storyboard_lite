# Scene 18: Wrap Up: Aftermath and Lou’s New Proxy

**Location:** Various: family home; Pavel’s locker home in Subjunction; Rosewater elevator  
**Time:** Later (summary period)  
**Characters:** Mara, Pavel, Gregor, Bettina, Lou (via remote robot), Lou’s Tray RB, Lou’s new caregiver robot

---

```text
SHOT TYPE: Medium two-shot inside a rising Rosewater elevator car, eye-level, slight three-quarter angle to capture profiles and reflections on the brushed c-plast walls.

SUBJECT: Mara in a practical Thrive enviro-suit—scuffed orange-gray fabric with reinforced seams, cooling lines visible at the collar, Brazzo-boot soles dusty, a compact tool-harness with curly pipe fasteners and a coil of flexible “curly” generator tubing clipped at her hip; salt-and-pepper hair in a neat shell shape; lightweight AR glasses perched low on her nose. Beside her stands Lou’s new caregiver proxy robot—human-scale, dressed in thrifted-but-clean trousers and soft flannel shirt, sleeves rolled once; articulated caregiver hands with soft synthetic pads; a smart-material face subtly morphing through empathetic micro-expressions; thin neck conduit and hidden joint seams peeking beneath the collar; discrete battery pack ridge under the shirt back; wearing understated, ultra-light AR eyewear like everyone else.

FOREGROUND: Elevator interior details of prosperous Thrive infrastructure—polished c-plast panels with hairline service seams, elevator status glyphs projected in faint AR over the door frame; a narrow vertical inspection slit revealing the lit shaft rushing by; a waist-high safety rail embossed with Rosewater’s maintenance codes; a mounted emergency battery exchange port; faint flecks of white rock dust on the floor from Mara’s morning installs; a coiled, thumb-thick flexible “curly” test segment clipped to Mara’s harness, labeled with temperature gradient arrows.

BACKGROUND: Through a narrow window and reflective door seam, glimpses of Detroit’s underground Rosewater atrium soaring past—broad circular terrace-roads stacked in rings; private residence entries with clean modern facades and hanging gardens spilling lush vegetation; artificial daylight panels casting warm, even light; tiny electric vehicles zipping along narrow transport corridors; citizens in cooling suits and varied AR glasses; autonomous work bots gliding along terrace edges; battery swap kiosks with bright indicator bands. Far below in a darker cutaway, older tunnel arteries show exposed pipes, damp concrete, and a line of tiny carts in a service corridor, hinting at the city’s patchwork of scarcity and abundance.

LIGHTING: Soft artificial daylight spilling in from the atrium side; cool elevator ceiling strips adding a clinical rim; subtle green-blue AR glow reflecting off glasses; warm bounce from the hanging gardens. Gentle highlight on the robot’s morphing face and Mara’s cheek, controlled contrast to keep both readable.

ATMOSPHERE: Clean, temperate Thrive air inside the elevator; faint moisture sheen on Mara’s boots from humid lower levels; a sense of upward motion and quiet efficiency; distant hum of transport systems; a calm, intimate pause between neighbors amid a bustling subterranean metropolis.

TEXTURE: Brushed c-plast walls with fine directional grain; matte-fabric enviro-suit with abrasion wear; rubberized tool grips; flexible, ribbed “curly” tubing with heat-reactive banding; soft flannel weave over the robot’s chassis, with subtle drape over hard geometry; synthetic skin of the smart face—slightly satin with micro-actuator ripples at the temples; faint dust on the floor and rail.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: surface cityscapes, open blue sky, conventional skyscraper skylines, flying cars, neon cyberpunk streets, uniform ruin, uniform hyper-futurism, medieval or retro interiors, gasoline vehicles, firearms, overt weapons, glossy androids with chrome faces, exposed gory elements, fantasy creatures, cluttered graffiti, 1980s tech, VR headsets covering faces, absent AR glasses, outdoor sunshine, deserts, oceans, wood cabin elevator, steampunk gears, excessive bloom, cartoonish proportions, low-resolution details, text overlays, watermarks.

STYLE:
**Color palette**
- A tightly controlled teal-and-deep-green monochrome scheme dominates every frame, with near-black shadows and muted blue-gray midtones.
- Warm accents (burnt orange, mustard, cream, and the occasional red or coral) are used sparingly, so they pop: the orange protest signs, the red "BANNED" glow, the orange anatomy panel in the news graphic.
- The limited palette is what makes the set feel like one world, even across very different scenes.

**Linework and rendering**
- Confident, clean ink outlines with flat or lightly cel-shaded color fills, closer to comics than to painting.
- Subtle grain and texture overlays that give a printed, slightly aged feel, like risograph or screen-print work.
- Soft atmospheric gradients and glow effects (the hologram figure, the speed-swirl lines around the parkour scenes) layered over the flat shapes.

**Character design**
- Stylized but readable faces with simple features and expressive brows, in the vein of European *bande dessinée* or mid-century editorial illustration, rather than anime or hyper-realism.
- Recurring props like wraparound visor goggles act as a visual shorthand for the world's tech.
- Robots are sleek, matte, minimal.

**Composition**
- Dynamic, cinematic framing: dramatic leaps, crowd scenes with depth, and figures staged against detailed industrial or arena backgrounds.
- The news-broadcast scenes use a flatter, poster-like layout with panel grids, bold condensed sans-serif headlines, and cream borders, evoking WPA posters or vintage infographics.
```
