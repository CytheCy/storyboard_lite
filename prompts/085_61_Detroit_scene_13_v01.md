```text
SHOT TYPE:
- Wide interior descent shot from within a massive industrial cargo elevator, slightly low angle, capturing a dense crowd of young people and the towering shaft walls of underground Detroit as the lift drops toward the Thrive-planned drainage canals

SUBJECT:
- Bettina and her three friends packed among dozens of youths on a repurposed heavy-duty excavation elevator; Bettina glances forward with tense composure while her friend in a liquid-silver dress grips her elbow; another friend wears AR glasses with animated tiger-stripe overlays that flow from brow to temple; a third has a reactive jacket that loops between hip-length and cropped, exposing midriff at its shortest; nearby stand Berto and two friends, Berto’s hair close-cut at sides and tall on top, giving a knowing smirk; all characters wear diverse protective or cooling layers under party-ready outfits, and every person in frame has some form of glasses—ranging from rugged goggle-style enviro eyewear to ultra-light AR frames with subtle HUD glows; many display survivor markers—scars, callused wrists, sunken cheeks, two gleaming prosthetic fingers—contrasting with expensive new clothes and synth gemstone accessories; small wearable batteries clip at belts and straps, cables threaded along garments; a pocket-sized robot companion peeks from a sling bag; everyone sways to descent jolts and low club bass felt through the floor

FOREGROUND:
- Padded cargo rails wrapped in soft upholstery, bolted portable speakers with scuffed housings, a safety placard with Thrive iconography and AI caution glyphs; Bettina’s hand tight around a rail, knuckles pale; AR overlays reflected in her ultra-light lenses—faint UI grids and icon pips; glints of synth gems on a young man’s collar catching light from rotating disco cubes; the reactive jacket in mid-transition, hem digitally rippling; fingertips momentarily stretching past the rail before an AI warning prompt projects as a translucent yellow ribbon: “Bring your arm in, please.”

BACKGROUND:
- Monolithic elevator shaft opening into a vast subterranean Detroit excavation: raw rock walls carved by gigantic excavation teeth with broad gouges and occasional protruding anchor bolts; high-tension industrial cables and heavy-duty roller wheels framed overhead; a ceiling canopy of shimmering panels masking the vertigo of the void above; scattered disco cubes and stringed utility lamps casting moving blue, green, and violet speckles across faces and rough stone; beyond the widening shaft, hints of the drainage network planned by Thrive AI—arched tunnel mouths, segmented culverts, catwalks with autonomous work bots trundling, and a distant canal trench with temporary floodgates and scaffolded platforms; side corridors reveal the city’s patchwork: muddy passages with dripping pipes and wire bundles leading to market niches, and a distant, well-lit Thrive terrace ring glimpsed through a maintenance viewport—clean circular roads, hanging gardens, artificial daylight spilling like a sun column; tiny electric carts glide along a lower transport corridor; salvaged robots parked near a battery exchange kiosk with stacked power cells

LIGHTING:
- Mixed: pulsing party light from rotating disco cubes (cool blues, greens, violets) dappled over damp rock and faces; dim amber utility strips along rail posts; sporadic inspection beams from maintenance drones; soft glow from AR glasses and HUDs on cheeks and lenses; the elevator interior is low-lit but rhythmically washed by colored reflections while warmer, sodium-like work lights flicker below near the canal works

ATMOSPHERE:
- A pressure of bodies, heat-shed from cooling suits, and breath fogging faintly in patches as cooler air rises from deeper levels; distant tremor of World Storm fading with descent; layered soundscape: portable speaker dance mix echoing up the shaft, undercut by a deep bass pulse emanating from the worksite or distant club spaces; intermittent PA announcements from a polite Thrive AI; droplets fall from condensation on the elevator canopy; the mood mixes giddy youth energy with survivor vigilance and the gravity of industrial scale

TEXTURE:
- Juxtaposition of slick and rough: cracked concrete, raw gouged stone, damp mineral sheen, frayed sound-dampening upholstery on rails; glossy synth gemstones, smooth carbon-fiber prosthetic digits, matte cooling fabric panels, reactive fiber weave of the morphing jacket; scuffed plastic housings on speakers, grease-smudged anchor bolts, braided cables with dust and condensation beads; AR glass lenses catching micro-scratches and luminous UI grain

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
- No surface city skylines or conventional futuristic skyscrapers; no flying cars or aerial traffic; no uniform ruin or uniformly pristine environments; no medieval or fantasy elements; no bright natural sunlight shafts from above; no retro 1950s sci-fi or cyberpunk neon cityscapes; no steampunk brass; no lifeless empty elevator; no glossy utopia; no weaponry or overt military; no outdated clothing without cooling or enviro logic; no VR headsets obscuring faces entirely; no brand logos; no dead robots or gore; no animals; no desert dunes or open oceans; no holographic billboards floating in mid-air outside practical AR lenses

STYLE:
**Color palette**
- A tightly controlled teal-and-deep-green monochrome scheme dominates every frame, with near-black shadows and muted blue-gray midtones.
- Warm accents (burnt orange, mustard, cream, and the occasional red or coral) are used sparingly, so they pop: the orange protest signs, the red "BANNED" glow, the orange anatomy panel in the news graphic.
- The limited palette is what makes the set feel like one world, even across very different scenes.

**Linework and rendering**
- Confident, clean ink outlines with flat or lightly cel-shaded color fills, closer to comics than to painting.
- Subtle grain and texture overlays that give a printed, slightly aged feel, like risograph or screen-print work.
- Soft atmospheric gradients and glow effects (the hologram figure, the speed-swirl lines around the parkour scenes) layered over the flat shapes.

**Character design**
- Stylized but readable faces with simple features and expressive brows, in the vein of European *bande dessinée* or mid-century editorial illustration, rather than anime or hyper-realism.
- Recurring props like wraparound visor goggles act as a visual shorthand for the world's tech.
- Robots are sleek, matte, minimal.

**Composition**
- Dynamic, cinematic framing: dramatic leaps, crowd scenes with depth, and figures staged against detailed industrial or arena backgrounds.
- The news-broadcast scenes use a flatter, poster-like layout with panel grids, bold condensed sans-serif headlines, and cream borders, evoking WPA posters or vintage infographics.
```
