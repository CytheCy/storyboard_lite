```text
SHOT TYPE: Medium-wide two-shot inside a rising elevator car, eye-level, slight three-quarter angle, capturing both figures from waist up with reflected views in brushed steel walls and glass panel revealing the vertical atrium shaft of underground Detroit sliding past.

SUBJECT: Mara in a practical Thrive-issued enviro-suit with cooling seams and dust-stained knees, utility belt with coil keys and curly pipe clamps, lightweight AR glasses perched low on her nose; beside her stands Lou’s newest remote caregiver bot wearing human trousers and soft flannel shirt, articulated caregiver’s hands folded, expressive smart-material face mid-blink, slim neck collar showing faint stasis-link indicators. Elevator control column with tactile dials, hand-scan pad, and a battery-credit meter ticking upward.

BACKGROUND: Through a narrow glass slit and overhead grille, the underground Detroit atrium races upward: concentric terrace-roads and multilevel galleries. Below: impoverished districts—cracked concrete, exposed pipes, dripping ceilings, muddy passages, crowded night markets lit by dim utility bulbs, scavengers pushing carts, salvaged robots hauling crates, people in mixed enviro/cooling suits and varied AR glasses. Higher levels transition into a Thrive neighborhood—broad circular terrace roads, multistory atriums strung with hanging gardens, misting irrigation, lush vegetation under artificial daylight panels, clean modern interiors with tiny electric vehicles gliding on ramps. Across the gap: Lou’s residence entrance ringed by terrace planters; his Tray RB guiding a tracked industrial cart; the new flannel-clad bot’s twin passes by with a battery caddy.

LIGHTING: Mixed industrial and horticultural—cool elevator LEDs overhead, warm spill from terrace markets below, soft daylight-spectrum panels bathing greenery above; intermittent strobe from maintenance beacons across the shaft creates gentle motion bands on faces; small instrument panes in the elevator glow cyan.

ATMOSPHERE: Humid, recycled air with fine dust motes lit in shafts; faint condensation along the elevator seam; subtle vibration hum; a sense of rising from scarcity into curated abundance while the city’s storms rumble muted far aboveground.

TEXTURE: Practical industrial realism—scratched steel, rubberized floor with tread wear, braided composite of curly tubes, flannel weave on the bot’s shirt, matte ceramic joints of the bot’s hands, damp concrete and corroded pipework below, smooth c-plast balustrades and plant leaves beaded with irrigation mist above.

STYLE:

Keep everything clearly drawn and illustrative, not photorealistic, not cinematic realism, and not a 3D render.
