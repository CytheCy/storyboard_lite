Technology

- Enviro-suit: Protective clothing worn for harsh environmental conditions, with hoods, zippers, and reinforced features.
- A.R. display / AR glasses: Augmented reality interfaces that float visuals before the eyes or as wearable glasses.
- House battery: Large rectangular home power unit that can be disconnected and exchanged to supply household electricity.
- Hauling bots: Small robots used to carry loads, which cost money to use.
- Main switch: A household power cutoff used during battery replacement.
- Hand truck: A manual wheeled device used to move the heavy house battery.
- Flatbed fallow cart: A flatbed transport cart used to carry the house battery.
- Tiny car: Small two-person or compact autonomous vehicles used on terrace rings and tunnels.
- Pallet jack: A lifting tool used by a neighbor to hoist a house battery onto a vehicle.
- Freight elevator: Large platform elevator for moving people and vehicles between levels.
- AR elements: Visual overlays occupying a user’s field of view during movement.
- Power generator (household): A device a household might own to generate its own electricity instead of battery exchanges.
- Curlies (curly power pipe/generator): Flexible tubes installed in rock beside a ventilation shaft to harvest temperature flux for electricity.
- Terrace ring: A circulating walkway/road system around an underground atrium used by pedestrians and vehicles.
- Assist: An AI helper that reads text aloud, records notes, and handles tasks via gaze/blink interaction.
- Virtual reality school: Education platform where students attend in VR environments.
- Personality clones (P-clones): AI duplicates of individuals that teach, take exams, simulate interactions, and later can mirror a person’s skills and personality.
- Compatible interface: Hardware needed to access VR schools and P-clones, sometimes limited by power/network issues.
- Interactive simulations/data environments: Tools for demonstrating subjects and examining research results in AR/VR.
- Text-to-speech: Technology allowing listening to written content.
- A.R. Two: An advanced AR system enabling users to perceive using sound-assisted spatial cues.
- Noise cancellation: Environmental audio control used to create or remove silence.
- Glasses with mirrored lenses: Eyewear reflecting outward, worn by a neighbor.
- Entryway garden systems: Residential planting installations along terraces and entrances.
- Immortal rats longevity setup: Experimental lab setup with monitoring equipment, treatment supplies, and AR displays to track biomarkers.
- AR display of biomarkers: Augmented reality graphs and images showing age markers, activity, and inflammation metrics.
- Protein-recycling treatment: A longevity intervention referenced for lab animals.
- Partial cellular reprogramming cycles: Timed treatments to reduce aging while managing growth risks.
- Stasis retirement services: Services providing long-term stasis as a form of retirement support.
- Stasis (general): Technology that slows or pauses bodily aging/metabolism for remote operation or long-term rest.
- Cam Mist: A dust-like material of micro-cameras forming a covert, distributed imaging system.
- Reconstruction software: Software that merges fragmented camera data into continuous video.
- Blind relays: Ubiquitous network relays that pass weak radio signals anonymously.
- Slip: An aerosolized hijack drug that boosts sexual drive and impairs self-control while preserving lucidity and memory.
- Flatbed tiny car: A tiny car variant configured with a flatbed for cargo.
- Printed undershirt/clothes: Garments produced by printing, implying on-demand fabrication.
- AR avatar (Kim): A narrated augmented persona that provides commentary in a user’s space.
- C-plast: Transparent or semi-transparent construction/plastic sheet material used as coverings and doors.
- Germination jar: A container used for sprouting/starting biological growth.
- Foldable tow boxes: Collapsible containers used for transporting goods.
- Enviro-jacket: Protective outerwear associated with enviro-suits.
- Dates Navigator: A matchmaking app/tool using deep psychology for relationship compatibility.
- Thrive (AI/system): An AI-driven life-management system handling work placement, guidance, matchmaking, and large-scale planning.
- Lutin 2: An earlier robot model requiring safe movement modes in unfamiliar environments.
- Tray RB: A new Thrive robot designed to assume work, chores, care, make maps rapidly, sense materials, and execute decisions to earn income for its owner.
- Local rapid mapping hardware: Onboard sensors/processors enabling Tray RB to map new spaces at full speed.
- Material-identifying sensors: Sensors that detect materials inside objects through layers to distinguish living and non-living things.
- Executive decision-making system (robotic): Tray RB’s capability to autonomously plan and act on Thrive’s guidance.
- Health and mobility monitoring at home: Tray RB’s in-home observation for changes in owner’s health and movement.
- Planting bed and fabric: Installed garden hardware including pinned planting fabric for soil containment.
- Rodent traps: Devices placed around terraces for pest control, maintained by the robot.
- Upcycle market: Marketplace for repaired/repurposed goods.
- Virtual jam spaces: Online environments for collaborative music improvisation.
- Congas, acoustic guitar, electric bass, keyboard, grand piano, harp, drum kit: Musical instruments used by Gregor in physical or virtual settings.
- P-clone baking/training: Long-term data collection and model training process to develop a personality clone.
- Virtual drum kit: Instrument interface used in a virtual environment.
- Female-variant P-clone transformation: Configurable change of a P-clone’s appearance and personality traits without disrupting continuity.
- Freight/party elevator with safety systems: Heavy-duty elevator refitted with upholstery, safety prompts, speakers, and lighting for club access.
- Portable speakers/disco cubes: Mounted audio and lighting devices in the elevator.
- Canal-boring machine: Massive excavation machine with cutting heads and teeth for underground canals.
- Drainage network and deep reservoir: Planned flood-control infrastructure designed by Thrive AI beneath Detroit.
- Security fences for club: Barriers enclosing the underground club area.
- Pleasure ship (virtual): A VR setting replicating a miniature ocean liner with interactive features.
- Notification system: Messaging/alert overlays within virtual environments.
- Industrial cart with wide treads: Heavy cart guided by a robot to move excavated rock.
