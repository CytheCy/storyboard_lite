- A glittering dust of hidden cameras, a breath of Slip, and a life destroyed—because consent can be hacked. 
- In Rosewater’s underground terraces, tech promises safety and prosperity—until love loops, jack-casts, and hijack drugs rewrite what “choice” means. 
- Tray RB can work, earn, and decide for you—freedom for some, quiet tyranny for others.
