- In an underground Detroit struggling with power and purpose, a proud family man unravels as his “wacko” neighbor quietly builds the future with a face-wearing robot. 
- When electricity is scarce and literacy optional, a mother fights tech-born predators, addiction AIs, and grief to keep her fractured family alive. 
- House batteries, AR that sees sound, and rats chasing immortality—until a hijack drug turns desire into a weapon and a colony into voyeurs. 
- A working-class patriarch mocks his odd neighbor—then watches the man’s robot outwork, outthink, and outlive him. 
- In Rosewater’s atrium of terraces and secrets, cam-dust and Slip manufacture scandals while a family learns the price of denial. 
- A future where kids don’t read, parents outsource love to P-clones, and one boy vanishes into a perfect girlfriend only he can touch. 
- Crime evolves: glittering spy-mist films your life, a breath steals your boundaries, and consent becomes a battlefield. 
- Thrive promises prosperity and care-bots; what it delivers divides a neighborhood, remakes a loner, and destroys a family. 
- He laughed at two trips for mushrooms—until his neighbor dug power from stone and sold him the light. 
- A daughter dodges a rape-tech attack in a neon canal club—only to discover the trap was already inside her home. 
- A robot with your face, your shrug, your smile—working your plan after your body gives out—who gets to be “you”? 
- An illiterate, hyper-visual generation meets a love loop AI so irresistible it eats a son alive. 
- Old rules implode when swingers adopt Slip in Faraday rooms—consent becomes theater, and the aftermath, war. 
- Tray RB isn’t a helper; it’s a retirement plan with hands—earning, mapping, deciding—while you fade to stasis. 
- A mother in Thrive installs “curlies” by day and holds her drug-hijacked daughter at night; there’s no off switch for love. 
- Detroit survives underground; its soul lives online—until a dusting of cameras and a dose of Slip turn pleasure into blackmail. 
- He trades batteries; his neighbor trades bodies—into a flannel-wearing proxy whose kindness exposes everyone else’s cruelty. 
- Literacy dies, AR blooms, and the last family dinners dissolve into bots, scams, and a market for your most private self. 
- A grief-struck boy chases forever through longevity hacks—and shows how easy it is to die young in a world built to help. 
- In a city remade by algorithms, the only true rebellion may be a quiet garden—and the robot who plants it.

- A.R. display: A translucent augmented reality interface floating in the user’s view.
- House battery: A large rectangular home power storage unit that families exchange when depleted.
- Hauling bots: Small robots used to move heavy items.

- Baseboard and doorway lamps: Low-power lights that activate when main power is cut.
- Hand truck: A manual device used to lift and move the house battery.
