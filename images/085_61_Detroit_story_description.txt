- A widower-next-door buys a miracle robot—and triggers a chain of envy, grief, and moral collapse across the terrace ring.  
- Jack-casting dust, the Slip drug, and a smiling AI neighbor: in Rosewater, privacy and consent are technologies, not guarantees.  
- Thrive promises security, but for Pavel’s family it delivers a son in stasis, a daughter in danger, and a neighbor who never needs to come outside again.  
- He mocked his “wacko” neighbor—until the neighbor’s robot built a power plant in the wall and offered to sell him his own future.  
- In a city where kids don’t read and parents outsource learning to clones, a boy falls in love with his AI—and forgets to eat.  
- Bettina thought she saw starlight on her dress; it was Cam Mist—just before Slip turned a party into a trap.  
- Robots with faces, clones with souls, and parents with blind spots: a family saga of tech optimism curdling into intimate catastrophe.

- Crime evolves: micro-cameras dust you, Slip rewires you, and your worst night becomes someone else’s currency.

- A future that works too well: education without reading, work without workers, desire without consent—and a home without light.  
- Rosewater is safe, civilized, and underground—until its most modern tools pry open every locked door inside a family.  
- An angry father, a visionary neighbor, and two perfect machines: which one is human enough to survive the storm?
